### **The Thesis: From 0s and 1s to **

Some environments demand more than participation. This is one of them.

In the startup world, the hardest part isn’t the building—it’s deciding what is worth building in the first place. **Codevolt 2.0** places you in that crucible. Over 3 Days, hundreds of builders work in parallel across disciplines and ideas, all attempting to answer the same hard questions in different ways.

Build a startup in 3 Days. From scratch. Addressing real problems with real solutions.

* **Format:** Teams only.
* **Availability:** Limited seating.

### **How it Works: 3 Days. 3 Gates. One Direction.**

The journey follows the full arc of early-stage startup building: problem selection, validation, trade-offs, and defense. **Not every team reaches the final day.**

* **Day 1: The Idea | *Clarity begins here.*** Identify a real problem, question every assumption, and—most importantly—decide what *not* to build.
* **Day 2: The Product | *Execution exposes reality.***
Turn intent into a functional MVP. Validate with real use cases and navigate difficult trade-offs.
* **Day 3: The Business | *Readiness matters.***
Define value, map your users/market, and present a working product.

---

### **The Rules of Reality: Evaluation**

We don't use scorecards or predefined checklists. Teams are evaluated the way early-stage startups are: through **decisions, clarity, and realism.**

| **Focus On** | **What Matters** | **What Doesn't** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Problem Clarity | Clear thinking > Complex features | Animated slides |
| User Understanding | Honest validation > Assumptions | Buzzwords & Trending Tech |
| Product Thinking | Direction > Speed | Overbuilding |
| Business Sense | Decision Ownership | AI-generated "decisions" |

> **A Note on AI:** AI can help you move faster, but it cannot decide for you. Teams relying on auto-generated ideas or copied concepts will be filtered out. We value the ability to defend *why* your product should exist.
