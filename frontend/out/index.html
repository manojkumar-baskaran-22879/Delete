How it Works: 3 Days. 3 Gates.
The journey follows the full arc of early-stage startup building: problem selection, validation, trade-offs, and defense.
Not every team reaches the final day.
1
Day 1: The Idea
Clarity begins here.
Identify a real problem, question every assumption, and, most importantly, decide what not to build.
2
Day 2: The Product
Execution exposes reality.
Turn intent into a functional MVP. Validate with real use cases and navigate difficult trade-offs.
3
Day 3: The Business
Readiness matters.
Define value, map your users/market, and present a working product.
The Builder’s Evaluation
We don't use scorecards or predefined checklists. Teams are evaluated the way early-stage startups are: through decisions, clarity, and execution.
A Note on AI: AI can help you move faster, but it cannot decide for you. Teams relying on AI-generated ideas or copied concepts will be disqualified.
